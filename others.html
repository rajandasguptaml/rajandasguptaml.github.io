Selected Research Projects

  • BRAINS: Retrieval-augmented multimodal framework for Alzheimer’s disease detection and interpretable clinical decision support.
  • HyCARD-Net: Hybrid intelligence framework for cardiovascular disease diagnosis using neural and interpretable components.
  • Locally Interpretable Neural Framework: Explainable modeling approach for liver cirrhosis staging.
  • ViMoNet: Multimodal vision–language framework for human-behavior understanding from motion and video.
  • VLAgeBench: Benchmarking framework for vision–language models on zero-shot human age estimation.
  • Multimodal Programming: Interactive CS-education assistant powered by large language models (HCII 2025).
  • Dzongkha–English Benchmark: Multilingual question-answering benchmark for low-resource foundation models.
