🧑 Career Summary


I am an aspiring AI and computer science researcher. I work on trustworthy, explainable, and human-centered AI for healthcare and clinical decision support, and I want that work to make healthcare more accessible. It runs along three connected directions:

  1. Retrieval-Augmented Clinical AI: Systems that combine external medical knowledge with multimodal reasoning to make diagnostic and monitoring tasks more reliable.
  2. Explainable and Interpretable ML: Prediction models whose decisions are transparent and locally interpretable, and that stay robust under distribution shift in healthcare settings.
  3. Multimodal and Vision-Language Reasoning: Models that reason jointly over text, image, motion, and tabular signals for clinical and human-centered applications, especially in low-resource and resource-constrained settings.

For my doctoral research, I propose to build reliable and interpretable AI systems for clinical decision support in resource-constrained healthcare settings, with emphasis on multimodal learning, retrieval-augmented reasoning, explainable prediction, and robust evaluation.

I am a Research Associate at ElITE Research Lab LLC (New York, USA / Remote), where I design and evaluate retrieval-augmented multimodal systems for clinical decision support. Before that I was a Research Assistant at Multimedia University (Malaysia), working on applied AI research, dataset construction, and reproducible evaluation pipelines, and a Researcher at American International University-Bangladesh, where I built interpretable ML models for clinical prediction tasks. I am also Product Manager, AI Systems at APEX DMIT Ltd., where I turn ML research into deployable AI systems.
I collaborate with Ts. Dr. Md. Jakir Hossen and Dr. Liew Tze Hui (Multimedia University) on retrieval-augmented clinical AI and multimodal vision-language reasoning; Prof. Dr. M. Firoz Mridha (AIUB) on explainable ML and LLM-assisted multimodal programming; Prof. Md. Kishor Morol (Cornell University) on multimodal clinical AI and low-resource NLP; and Dr. Salah Uddin Ahmed (USN / NTNU, Norway) on LLM-assisted programming and human-centered AI.
My papers have appeared at EMNLP (CORE A*), ICMLA, HCII, ICCIT, IJPHS, MECON, ICICML, ICHIH, AIBDF, ICON-SONICS, and ICHORA. I review for PLOS ONE, IEEE SMC, ICONIP, and IEEE EDUCON, and I have served as a judge for the Three Minute Thesis Competition. I have led research projects from problem formulation through to revision. I pick up new topics quickly and enjoy sharing what I learn.
